# Turkey, Worked Out. — image brief (8 plates)

Download in this order. Hand the files back named EXACTLY as column 1 —
`A01.jpg`, `A02.jpg`. The slot is what the build wires against; a file named
`bazaar.jpg` has to be matched by hand and gets matched wrong.

**Register:** reasoning, not warning. No faces, no brand marks, no shop signage,
no readable documents, plates, prices or rate boards. Nothing that reads as
distress, damage or enforcement. These pages argue; a photograph that looks like
a warning changes what the book is.

**Shape is non-negotiable: 3:2 LANDSCAPE, 1600px or wider.** The text column is
6.8in — a 3:2 fills it at 4.5in tall (49% of the text block). A 2:3 portrait would
need 10.2in, taller than the page, and capping it leaves a ~200px stamp in 480px
of cream. The build refuses portraits outright.

Unsplash and Pexels only. Confirm the licence on each file before it ships in a
paid product.

## Flagged — where the search will fight you

- **`A01`** — results are dominated by balloon close-ups at sunrise. That is the
  postcard and it argues the opposite of this cover: intimacy, where the page
  needs scale. Filter for landscape with an empty foreground; if nothing clean
  appears, a wide Anatolian steppe or a coastline works better than a famous view.
- **`A02`** — the hard one. Any legible number destroys the point, which is that
  the answer is not posted anywhere. Look for boards shot at an angle, out of
  focus, or from far enough that text does not resolve.
- **`A04`** — must be taken FROM distance, not from a basket. Passenger shots
  bring faces and argue proximity; this slot argues that the sky decides.
- **`A05`** — searches drift to plated restaurant dishes. A plated dish argues for
  a recommendation this book explicitly refuses to make. The counter, with trays,
  is the shot.
- **`A07`** — anything with 'road' plus 'Turkey' pulls in accident and hazard
  imagery. The chapter lists what goes wrong; the photograph must not.

## After download

Confirm 8 files, not 16 — macOS writes `._` AppleDouble twins when copying to
FAT/exFAT or a network share. `dot_clean .` or `find . -name '._*' -delete`
first, and `export COPYFILE_DISABLE=1` in `~/.zshrc` to stop it recurring.

Each file is opened and checked at the pixel before any caption is written. The
Thailand build committed captions from filenames and put a Bangkok river shot on
a chapter about island ferries. A caption that lies is worse than no photograph —
a file that does not argue its slot gets re-sourced, not captioned to fit.
