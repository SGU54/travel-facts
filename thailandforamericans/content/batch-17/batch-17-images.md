# batch-17-images.md — download manifest

**7 images, in this exact order.** `upload-batch.mjs` maps them by download order, so keep
them numbered as listed. Save them into one folder, named `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, … `07.jpg`.

```
node scripts/upload-batch.mjs <images-folder> import-batch-17.json
```

**Before you save any image, check all four:** landscape, ≥1600px wide, Unsplash/Pexels
license (free commercial, no attribution), and **no recognizable faces and no brand or bank
logo signage**. The logo rule bites hardest on slots 4 and 5 — an ATM photo with a readable
bank brand is a reject.

The links are **pages to pick from**, not direct downloads.
