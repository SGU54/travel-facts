This sheet expires. The next one is free.

Issued 2026-10-14. Reissued every quarter. To pull the current one, go to vietnamforamericans.com/guide/sheet and enter the email you bought the manual with.

This sheet is the short half of a two-document set. The book argues the decisions and is built to stay true for about two years; this sheet carries the numbers those arguments run on and is reissued every quarter. Print it, or keep it on the phone — it is the half you want with you at a counter.

Checked 2026-10-14. Each row names the source it was read from, as a clickable address, and the decision it can reverse. A row marked confirm in your travel week is a figure no primary source publishes as a schedule: the sheet tells you where to look rather than printing a number it cannot stand behind.
