## The day arithmetic — what asking for it explicitly bought

The gate brief said *"DO THE ARITHMETIC ON ITS DAY LIST EXPLICITLY"*, and it did. My twenty-one-day
route was wrong in three ways at once and none of them was visible from reading it:

1. **"Three internal flights"** stated twice, where the route describes two. Sapa and Ninh Binh are
   road trips from Hanoi and the delta is a road trip from Ho Chi Minh City; nothing added a third.
2. **Overlapping day ranges** — "Day 12, fly to Hue. Days 12 to 13, Hue", and the same at Hoi An and
   Ho Chi Minh City. Rewritten as twenty-one non-overlapping days that actually total twenty-one.
3. **Two delta nights advertised, one available.** Days 20–21 ending in a departure accommodate one.
   Now stated as one night, with the dawn market on day 21 and a warning to check the flight time.

Also: the "additions" were presented as things three weeks gives you, when each is a **substitution**
— a beach ending replaces the delta days, Ha Giang replaces Sapa and wants more, Con Dao costs four
or five days because of its flight schedule. And *"the ability to lose two days to a typhoon"* when
the route has no spare days at all. Now: if you want contingency, leave a day unbooked in Hanoi and
another in Hoi An.

**Lesson: when an article contains a numbered plan, ask the gate to add it up.** It is a check I
cannot reliably do by eye on my own prose.

## Licence compliance — a gap the brief's PARAPHRASE/GLOSS/DIRECTION test caught

correction 2 requires**: that renting without a Vietnamese licence is illegal, that driving without

sentence also said the licence position *"means riding pillion"*, which is too exclusive — a car
with a driver covers the same route.
