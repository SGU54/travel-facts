## The vaccination finding — the gloss problem again, third batch running

The span was correct. The sentence around it was not:

> *"The CDC recommends Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, and typhoid **for most travelers** travelling to
> Vietnam"*

The fact distinguishes three different scopes — **Hepatitis A** for unvaccinated travellers aged
one and over, **Hepatitis B** for unvaccinated travellers of all ages, **typhoid** for most
travellers. My sentence applied the last qualifier to all three and dropped the vaccination-status
and age conditions. Now stated explicitly, each with its own scope.

Also corrected: *"This is not something to arrange the week before the flight"* — the gate noted
CDC has measures for travellers departing in under two weeks, so discouraging a late appointment
is wrong advice. Now: book early for more options, but a late appointment is still worth making.
