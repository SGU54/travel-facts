# Decision gaps — choices DK 2015 describes but never resolves

Dossier material. A guidebook lays options side by side; it almost never says "for *this* traveller, do *that*, because…". Each entry below names the fork, what the book says about each prong, and the synthesis a decision manual would owe the reader. Page refs are DK 2015. Facts needed to resolve a fork are leads until verified.

## Routing and sequence
1. **Fly in HAN, out SGN — or the reverse?** The book's two-week plan is open-jaw north→south (p12) and gives no reason. It never weighs: Tet-season flight pricing by gateway, which end has the weather at your dates, jet-lag direction, where the US nonstop lands (SFO–SGN only). → our north-south-routing swamp; the dossier should give a rule keyed to month + gateway.
2. **Train vs car-and-driver on the central coast.** The book switches from train to car after Halong because the railway misses My Son and Mui Ne (p12), then separately calls the train "most efficient" (p282) and car "relatively inexpensive" (p280). Never reconciled: per-person cost at party sizes 1/2/4, time, and which stops only a car reaches.
3. **Open Tour bus vs point-to-point.** "Most popular with tourists… cheap and flexible" (p283) vs express buses "faster, safer, more comfortable" (p283). No verdict on who should take which. Today the fork is sleeper bus vs train vs flight per leg; the book predates app booking.
4. **Highway 1 vs Highway 14 (coast vs inland Ho Chi Minh road).** Raised only for cyclists (p266): Highway 1 congested and flood-prone, 14 "picturesque". The same fork exists for drivers and is never discussed for them.
5. **Hai Van Pass vs tunnel vs train between Hue and Danang.** Pass = "most spectacular views" (p126); train "also" serves the region (p127). No guidance on mode, weather, or whether to break the journey at Lang Co ("one of the best beaches in the region", p126).
6. **Laos overland: which gate, or fly?** Seven crossings listed, "extremely time-consuming… advised to fly" (p281). No ranking of gates, no sleeper-bus assessment.
7. **Cambodia: Moc Bai bus vs Mekong boat via Vinh Xuong.** Both described (p281), the boat "more scenic". Never costed in time or money, never tied to whether you want the Delta anyway.

## Bases and side-trips
8. **Hue as base for the north-of-pass, Hoi An/Danang for the south (p126).** Stated as geography, not as a decision: how many nights each, and whether Danang itself deserves a night. Our hoian-base-choice swamp.
9. **Halong: hire a boat at Bai Chay vs book in Hanoi (p185).** Price vs quality vs time never compared; overnight vs day never addressed. Our halong-bay-cruise-choice swamp — the book is too early to help.
10. **Sapa: night train vs road (p185), agency trek vs local guide (p265).** Book names agencies and says guides are "ambassadors" into villages; it never says when an independent trek is fine or what a homestay costs you in comfort. Our sapa-trek-choice swamp.
11. **Mekong: public boat vs private tour vs coach day-trip (p91).** "Private offers more comfort"; nothing on depth (My Tho day vs Can Tho overnight vs Chau Doc onward to Cambodia). Our mekong-day-vs-overnight swamp.
12. **Perfume Pagoda: festival crush (Feb–May) vs empty off-season (p34).** Atmosphere vs crowds never weighed.
13. **Nha Trang vs Mui Ne vs the quiet northern beaches (Quy Nhon, Doc Let) (p108–109).** Book: resorts are the bases, fishing towns "worthwhile stopovers". No rule for who should skip the resorts.
14. **Dalat detour: yes or no.** "Cool delight" (p107) but off the coastal line; cost in days never stated.
15. **Cu Chi with or without the Cao Dai Holy See (p11).** Offered as an extension; the noon-mass timing constraint and the full-day cost never stated.
16. **Ha Giang vs Sapa** — the book cannot see it; the dossier must.

## Timing
17. **March–May "best" (p270) vs the regional calendar (p38).** The book's own climate chart contradicts its one-line advice for the central coast. A decision manual needs a month × region grid with a verdict per cell; ours exists in geo.ts, but the *verdict* layer is dossier work.
18. **Travel during Tet or around it.** Book: "best for participating… prices higher… businesses shut up to a week" (p34, p270). Never says: for a first-timer with 10 days, is being there for Tet a net gain? Our tet-timing swamp.
19. **Rainy-season discount vs flood/visibility risk (p270).** Stated as a trade; never quantified or mapped to places where rain matters (Halong fog, Sapa mud) vs where it doesn't (HCMC afternoon showers).
20. **Peak flight booking lead-time (3 months, p270).** Why that window and whether it applies to US origins is unexplained.

## Money and services
21. **Taxi vs xe om vs walking (p284).** "Safest" vs "quickest and cheapest"; the book never gives a per-situation rule (luggage, night, rain, solo woman). Today Grab mostly resolves it; the residual fork is Grab vs street taxi at airports and in rain.
22. **Hotel: walk-in bargaining vs booking ahead (p232, p234).** Book says both ("advance booking advisable" and "walk-in rates negotiable up to 30%"). Never resolved by season or city.
23. **Homestay vs guesthouse (p234).** Homestay = "invaluable experience"; comfort, privacy and bathroom reality never stated.
24. **Pay in USD or dong (p254, p276).** Book: always dong, cheaper — but also says USD accepted everywhere. Legal position and today's practice (dong only) resolve it; the book doesn't.
25. **Exchange at bank vs private counter vs gold shop (p276).** Rates vs recourse described; no rule by amount.
26. **ATM: many small withdrawals vs few large ones (p276).** Book gives cap and fee figures; never draws the conclusion (fewer, larger withdrawals). corrections #1 states the rule for us.
27. **Tip or not: hotel (p235) vs restaurant (p243) advice diverge** ("US$1 generous" vs "10% appreciated"). No single frame for an American who tips 20% at home.
28. **Car-and-driver pricing (p283): what drives the US$65–120 range.** Distance and fuel named; never worked into a per-day decision for a given itinerary.

## Risk and rules
29. **Motorbike: rent, xe om, or don't (p283–285).** Book: licence "rarely asked", xe om "best", self-drive car "never". It contradicts itself across pages and never addresses insurance validity — the thing that actually decides it (facts.json getting_around.unlicensed_insurance). Our motorbike-or-driver swamp.
30. **Passport to the hotel or a photocopy (p233).** Both described as acceptable; no rule for when to insist on the copy.
31. **Street food: "isn't always risky… use judgement" (p275).** No decision rule (cooked-to-order, crowd, time of day, which dishes are riskiest).
32. **Malaria prophylaxis for Mekong/Phu Quoc (p271, p275)** — the book says maybe; CDC says the deltas are rare and cities nil. Verdict belongs to CDC, but the dossier should state the decision plainly.
33. **Exotic-meat menus (p245).** Book lists them; never tells a squeamish reader how to avoid them by vocabulary.
34. **Shipping purchases home vs carrying (p278–279).** Inspection delays and courier searches described; no rule by item (tailored clothes, lacquer, furniture).

## What the book settles well (keep, cite as book opinion)
- Never self-drive a car (p285). Agrees with the fact layer.
- Crossing the street: steady pace, no stopping (p285).
- Dong, not dollars (p254).
- Pay-in-advance medical reality and medevac insurance (p274).
- Central coast is its own weather regime (p38 chart, despite the text).

# Lonely Planet 2016 — additional unresolved forks (2026-10-16)

Numbering continues from the DK list. Only forks LP raises that DK did not, or where LP takes the opposite side.

## Routing and sequence
35. **South→north or north→south — the books disagree.** LP's coastal classic runs HCMC→Hanoi (p28); DK's runs Hanoi→HCMC (p12). Neither gives a reason tied to month, gateway or jet lag. The swamp is confirmed real: two major editors default opposite ways.
36. **Hue: one night (LP p29) or two days (DK p12)?** LP gives Hoi An three days and Hue one; DK gives Hue two. No criterion offered.
37. **Phong Nha on the spine or skipped?** LP routes Hue→Phong Nha→Halong; DK doesn't mention it. Cost: a day and a half. Neither weighs it against Halong or Ninh Binh.
38. **Open-tour ticket vs point-to-point tickets (LP p493).** LP states the trade (cheaper, central departures; herded, kickbacks, locked to the trail) and ends "many people go for them". No rule by trip length or style.
39. **Ha Giang: loop by hired bike, xe om pillion, or car (LP p31)?** No public transport on the key stretch; LP lists the three options without a verdict and without the licence/insurance problem.
40. **Bus at 50 km/h vs sleeper train vs flight per leg (LP p492, p356).** LP's own table shows flying ≈ sleeper price beyond Nha Trang; it never says "fly those legs".
41. **Fly a day early for international connections (LP p491).** LP says regional cancellations are common and advises a buffer day, but not for which airports or seasons.

## Timing
42. **Which city's calendar wins when they conflict (LP region openers).** Hanoi Oct–Dec, Hue May/Sep, Nha Trang July, Mui Ne December, HCMC Feb–Mar, Phu Quoc January. A two-city trip has no single best month; LP never says how to choose.
43. **August: peak and "hot, hot, hot" (LP p27) — go or avoid?** It is the only summer month most Americans can travel. LP describes, doesn't advise.
44. **Tet's nine-day train blackout (LP p497).** Stated as fact; never turned into "if your dates touch Tet, fly or stay put".
45. **Sapa harvest: September (reality) vs November (LP p99).** The book's own calendar contradicts the trekking season; a visitor needs a date.

## Bases and activities
46. **Sapa (crowded, dramatic) vs Bac Ha (quiet, less dramatic) vs Mai Chau (easy, valley) (LP p37).** Three bases ranked on different axes, no verdict for a first-timer with three days.
47. **Nha Trang beach vs Doc Let / Bai Dai (LP p28).** "Less developed base" offered as an aside; no rule on who should leave the city.
48. **Dive in Nha Trang (cheap, dodgy shops) or Con Dao (best, double price, remote) (LP p42).** Prices and quality given; the decision isn't.
49. **Easy Rider pillion vs self-ride vs car for Dalat→Mui Ne / Hai Van (LP p32, p39).** LP says "ideally on the back of a motorbike"; insurance validity decides it and LP buries that in another chapter.
50. **Street-food tour on night one vs a showcase restaurant first (LP p44).** Both suggested; no sequence.

## Money and services
51. **Three tipping opinions (LP p21, p482; DK p243).** None cites a source; the dossier owes a single rule.
52. **Who pays the driver's fuel (LP p494 vs DK p283).** Must be negotiated; neither book says that outright.
53. **Hand over the passport, or a photocopy (LP p478, DK p233).** Both say "photocopy at minimum"; neither says when to insist.
54. **Buy bus tickets at the station or through the hotel (LP p493 vs p94).** LP says both: station prices are fixed and drivers overcharge, but agents' commission "offsets the language hassle". No rule.
55. **Reconvert dong on departure or spend it down (LP p482).** "Cannot legally take dong out" vs small reconversion windows; no practical threshold.

## Risk
56. **Cyclos: charming (Hanoi) or mugging risk (HCMC, day only) (LP p95, p358, p495).** City-specific verdicts never consolidated.
57. **Malaria tablets for the Mekong (LP p500 "rural south high risk" vs CDC "rare in the deltas").** The two sources differ in emphasis; a traveller doing a two-day delta tour needs a yes/no.
58. **State hospital vs private clinic vs fly out (LP p499).** Tiered, but no threshold for "fly to Bangkok".

## What LP settles well (keep, cite as book opinion)
- Never rely on a regional flight for a same-day international connection.
- Direct international buses only at land borders.
- Buy bus tickets at the office, never from the driver.
- Upper berth is cheapest on hard sleepers; soft sleeper is one price.
- Ice in cities is fine; tap water is not.
- A car with driver makes Vietnam accessible to wheelchair users; trains do not.

# Moon Vietnam 2015 — additional forks and resolutions (2026-10-17)

Numbering continues. Moon is the most opinionated of the three books, so several earlier forks gain a verdict here (listed at the end); the new forks are the ones Moon raises or splits further.

## Routing and sequence
59. **Halong OR Ninh Binh for a short trip (p97).** Moon states it as the real either/or for travellers without four spare days; neither DK nor LP frames them as substitutes. Criteria never given: boat vs land, weather risk (Halong cancels in winter fog, Ninh Binh doesn't), cost.
60. **Hue: zero (Moon), one night (LP) or two days (DK)?** Moon's two-week spine skips Hue entirely in favour of three Hoi An days. The dossier owes a rule keyed to interest in history vs beach/food.
61. **Phong Nha's 4–5-day cost (p145) vs what it displaces.** Moon is the first to price it in days; it never says what to drop (Nha Trang? the Mekong?).
62. **Day cruise vs overnight at Halong, in days not dollars (p97).** Moon's arithmetic: a day cruise still costs two travel days from Hanoi. The question becomes "if you are spending two days anyway, why not sleep aboard?" — unanswered.
63. **Halong→Danang: night train or budget flight (p15).** Moon calls them equal in price and leaves the choice to whether you want another Hanoi night. Today's fares have moved; the frame (sleep on the train vs an extra city night) survives.
64. **Which Delta axis (p19–21, 391).** Can Tho/Cai Rang (the standard) vs the Vinh Long–Sa Dec–Tram Chim–Chau Doc line vs Tra Vinh's Khmer culture. Moon lays out all three and gives day counts but no selection rule by interest (markets, birds, culture, Cambodia onward).
65. **Phu Quoc minimum stay (p391, 460).** Moon: 2–3 days minimum because the flight or speedboat makes a short stay wasteful, and solo travellers pay a premium. Whether a couple's three Phu Quoc nights beat three Con Dao nights is never addressed.

## Timing
66. **Three national "best month" answers.** DK Mar–May; LP region-dependent; Moon Sep–Oct or Apr–May. The disagreement itself is the finding: any article giving one answer is wrong somewhere.
67. **Tet: participate (DK) or avoid (Moon "poor visit")?** Moon is the dissenting vote on fork #18. The dossier should state the trade for a 10-day first-timer explicitly.
68. **Halong's winter mist: atmosphere or cancellation risk (p98).** Moon says cruises get sidelined Dec–Feb; DK and LP sell the mist as moody. A reader with fixed February dates needs the cancellation probability, which no book has.

## Money and services
69. **Street cart vs restaurant for the same dish (p510).** Moon: you pay ten times for atmosphere and the food is often worse. True for locals; for a nervous first-timer the fork is really about hygiene confidence, which Moon handles separately (p506). The two need joining.
70. **Which taxi brand, and whether to phone one at night (p361).** Moon: Mai Linh/Vinasun always at night, others by day. Today the fork is street brand vs Grab vs Xanh SM; the "night" axis is the durable part.
71. **Xe om from the airport for solo travellers (p360).** A Moon-only option (cheap, 25 minutes, no luggage). Today it is a Grab bike. Never weighed against arriving tired with a bag.

## Risk
72. **Traffic stop: pay or refuse (p497).** Moon says refusing is a bad idea. We will not advise paying; the only clean resolution is not to ride unlicensed. This fork should be stated and closed in the dossier, not left open.
73. **Right turn on red: Moon says legal outside Hanoi (p495, 75); LP says not in cities.** Needs the current traffic law. Matters for anyone who does ride.
74. **Dune driving at Mui Ne vs residual landmines (p229).** Moon mentions remnants around Phan Thiet/Mui Ne; no book connects it to the quad-bike and jeep tours sold on the dunes.

## Forks from the earlier lists that Moon resolves (as book opinion)
- **#30 / #53 passport to the hotel** → hand it over for registration, ask for it back, expect to prepay (p497).
- **#31 street food rule** → clean, cooked-to-order, visible kitchen, herbs optional, ice fine in decent places (p506).
- **#33 exotic-meat vocabulary** → thit cho, hot vit lon, chuot dong, ran (p500).
- **#34 ship or carry** → courier, never the post (p511).
- **#45 Sapa harvest month** → September–October (p78), against LP's November.
- **#52 who pays the driver's fuel** → agree every fee before leaving; no default (p494).
- **#9 / Halong booking** → month verdict Sep–early Dec (p98); still nothing on Bai Chay vs Hanoi booking.
- **#21 taxi vs xe om** → xe om by day, taxi by night, never bargain a meter (p493–494).
- **#27 / #51 tipping** → Moon's fourth opinion (not customary except spas) does not resolve it; it confirms there is no norm to cite.

## What Moon settles well (keep, cite as book opinion)
- Halong: September to early December.
- Central coast: February to May; avoid September to January.
- Never bargain with a metered taxi; walk a block from any station before taking one.
- Taxi, not xe om, after dark.
- Order tailoring on the first Hoi An day.
- Phu Quoc needs three days to justify the hop.
- Simplify your English; agree the price before any service.
