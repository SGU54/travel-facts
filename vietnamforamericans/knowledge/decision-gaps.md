# Decision gaps — choices DK 2015 describes but never resolves

Dossier material. A guidebook lays options side by side; it almost never says "for *this* traveller, do *that*, because…". Each entry below names the fork, what the book says about each prong, and the synthesis a decision manual would owe the reader. Page refs are DK 2015. Facts needed to resolve a fork are leads until verified.

## Routing and sequence
1. **Fly in HAN, out SGN — or the reverse?** The book's two-week plan is open-jaw north→south (p12) and gives no reason. It never weighs: Tet-season flight pricing by gateway, which end has the weather at your dates, jet-lag direction, where the US nonstop lands (SFO–SGN only). → our north-south-routing swamp; the dossier should give a rule keyed to month + gateway.
2. **Train vs car-and-driver on the central coast.** The book switches from train to car after Halong because the railway misses My Son and Mui Ne (p12), then separately calls the train "most efficient" (p282) and car "relatively inexpensive" (p280). Never reconciled: per-person cost at party sizes 1/2/4, time, and which stops only a car reaches.
3. **Open Tour bus vs point-to-point.** "Most popular with tourists… cheap and flexible" (p283) vs express buses "faster, safer, more comfortable" (p283). No verdict on who should take which. Today the fork is sleeper bus vs train vs flight per leg; the book predates app booking.
4. **Highway 1 vs Highway 14 (coast vs inland Ho Chi Minh road).** Raised only for cyclists (p266): Highway 1 congested and flood-prone, 14 "picturesque". The same fork exists for drivers and is never discussed for them.
5. **Hai Van Pass vs tunnel vs train between Hue and Danang.** Pass = "most spectacular views" (p126); train "also" serves the region (p127). No guidance on mode, weather, or whether to break the journey at Lang Co ("one of the best beaches in the region", p126).
6. **Laos overland: which gate, or fly?** Seven crossings listed, "extremely time-consuming… advised to fly" (p281). No ranking of gates, no sleeper-bus assessment.
7. **Cambodia: Moc Bai bus vs Mekong boat via Vinh Xuong.** Both described (p281), the boat "more scenic". Never costed in time or money, never tied to whether you want the Delta anyway.

## Bases and side-trips
8. **Hue as base for the north-of-pass, Hoi An/Danang for the south (p126).** Stated as geography, not as a decision: how many nights each, and whether Danang itself deserves a night. Our hoian-base-choice swamp.
9. **Halong: hire a boat at Bai Chay vs book in Hanoi (p185).** Price vs quality vs time never compared; overnight vs day never addressed. Our halong-bay-cruise-choice swamp — the book is too early to help.
10. **Sapa: night train vs road (p185), agency trek vs local guide (p265).** Book names agencies and says guides are "ambassadors" into villages; it never says when an independent trek is fine or what a homestay costs you in comfort. Our sapa-trek-choice swamp.
11. **Mekong: public boat vs private tour vs coach day-trip (p91).** "Private offers more comfort"; nothing on depth (My Tho day vs Can Tho overnight vs Chau Doc onward to Cambodia). Our mekong-day-vs-overnight swamp.
12. **Perfume Pagoda: festival crush (Feb–May) vs empty off-season (p34).** Atmosphere vs crowds never weighed.
13. **Nha Trang vs Mui Ne vs the quiet northern beaches (Quy Nhon, Doc Let) (p108–109).** Book: resorts are the bases, fishing towns "worthwhile stopovers". No rule for who should skip the resorts.
14. **Dalat detour: yes or no.** "Cool delight" (p107) but off the coastal line; cost in days never stated.
15. **Cu Chi with or without the Cao Dai Holy See (p11).** Offered as an extension; the noon-mass timing constraint and the full-day cost never stated.
16. **Ha Giang vs Sapa** — the book cannot see it; the dossier must.

## Timing
17. **March–May "best" (p270) vs the regional calendar (p38).** The book's own climate chart contradicts its one-line advice for the central coast. A decision manual needs a month × region grid with a verdict per cell; ours exists in geo.ts, but the *verdict* layer is dossier work.
18. **Travel during Tet or around it.** Book: "best for participating… prices higher… businesses shut up to a week" (p34, p270). Never says: for a first-timer with 10 days, is being there for Tet a net gain? Our tet-timing swamp.
19. **Rainy-season discount vs flood/visibility risk (p270).** Stated as a trade; never quantified or mapped to places where rain matters (Halong fog, Sapa mud) vs where it doesn't (HCMC afternoon showers).
20. **Peak flight booking lead-time (3 months, p270).** Why that window and whether it applies to US origins is unexplained.

## Money and services
21. **Taxi vs xe om vs walking (p284).** "Safest" vs "quickest and cheapest"; the book never gives a per-situation rule (luggage, night, rain, solo woman). Today Grab mostly resolves it; the residual fork is Grab vs street taxi at airports and in rain.
22. **Hotel: walk-in bargaining vs booking ahead (p232, p234).** Book says both ("advance booking advisable" and "walk-in rates negotiable up to 30%"). Never resolved by season or city.
23. **Homestay vs guesthouse (p234).** Homestay = "invaluable experience"; comfort, privacy and bathroom reality never stated.
24. **Pay in USD or dong (p254, p276).** Book: always dong, cheaper — but also says USD accepted everywhere. Legal position and today's practice (dong only) resolve it; the book doesn't.
25. **Exchange at bank vs private counter vs gold shop (p276).** Rates vs recourse described; no rule by amount.
26. **ATM: many small withdrawals vs few large ones (p276).** Book gives cap and fee figures; never draws the conclusion (fewer, larger withdrawals). corrections #1 states the rule for us.
27. **Tip or not: hotel (p235) vs restaurant (p243) advice diverge** ("US$1 generous" vs "10% appreciated"). No single frame for an American who tips 20% at home.
28. **Car-and-driver pricing (p283): what drives the US$65–120 range.** Distance and fuel named; never worked into a per-day decision for a given itinerary.

## Risk and rules
29. **Motorbike: rent, xe om, or don't (p283–285).** Book: licence "rarely asked", xe om "best", self-drive car "never". It contradicts itself across pages and never addresses insurance validity — the thing that actually decides it (facts.json getting_around.unlicensed_insurance). Our motorbike-or-driver swamp.
30. **Passport to the hotel or a photocopy (p233).** Both described as acceptable; no rule for when to insist on the copy.
31. **Street food: "isn't always risky… use judgement" (p275).** No decision rule (cooked-to-order, crowd, time of day, which dishes are riskiest).
32. **Malaria prophylaxis for Mekong/Phu Quoc (p271, p275)** — the book says maybe; CDC says the deltas are rare and cities nil. Verdict belongs to CDC, but the dossier should state the decision plainly.
33. **Exotic-meat menus (p245).** Book lists them; never tells a squeamish reader how to avoid them by vocabulary.
34. **Shipping purchases home vs carrying (p278–279).** Inspection delays and courier searches described; no rule by item (tailored clothes, lacquer, furniture).

## What the book settles well (keep, cite as book opinion)
- Never self-drive a car (p285). Agrees with the fact layer.
- Crossing the street: steady pace, no stopping (p285).
- Dong, not dollars (p254).
- Pay-in-advance medical reality and medevac insurance (p274).
- Central coast is its own weather regime (p38 chart, despite the text).
