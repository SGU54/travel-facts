## C. Topics where the book and our corpus overlap and the book adds a usable angle

- **traveling-vietnam-during-tet**: book adds Kitchen God / week-before mechanics, human chess, firecracker ban (p32–33) and the "Viet Kieu fly home → book flights 3 months out" rule (p270).
- **vietnam-festivals-calendar**: ~25 named festivals with lunar months (seasons.json) — many more than we list; each a lead for the date.
- **best-time-to-visit-vietnam**: the book's own contradiction (p38 "dry Nov–Feb" vs its Hue chart) is a teachable example for the "there is no one dry season" thesis.
- **crossing-the-street**: book's rule matches ours word for word in substance (p285).
- **is-vietnam-safe-for-americans**: adds the District 1 "coffee invitation" card-game con (p274) and the Noi Bai hotel-diversion scam (p281).
- **vietnam-etiquette-for-americans**: palm-down beckoning, head-touching, chopsticks-upright, eldest-eats-first, baby-pinching, kinship address terms (culture-etiquette.json).
- **vietnam-war-history-sites-guide**: the "China is the historic enemy, the American War is one chapter" framing (history-context.json) and the fake-Zippo/dog-tag relic trade (p255).
- **hue-imperial-city-guide**: 1968 Tet Offensive 25-day occupation explains the ruin; Vauban + feng shui citadel design.
- **vegetarian-vegan-food-vietnam**: Hue as the Buddhist vegetarian capital; nuoc mam trap.
- **hanoi-old-quarter-guide**: tube-house land-deed origin of the skinny buildings; guild-street names.
- **ninh-binh / best-national-parks**: Cuc Phuong butterfly season (Apr–May), unmarked trails → guide, Muong village overnight.
