# Mercè Oller — LA MOSAÏQUE · the modular designer

**Who.** Came to print from exhibition design; thinks in panels. Believes a page is
assembled, the way a trencadís wall is assembled from fragments: each tile holds one
unit of information and takes the colour of its job, and the grout between tiles is
the only rule the page needs. Her sentence: "One tile, one fact. If a tile needs a
heading, it is two tiles."

**Her method, chapter by chapter.**
1. The six-column grid with an 8px grout; tiles span 2, 3, 4 or 6 columns; every row
   has a fixed height, so the page is composed, not flowed. Nothing is boxed; nothing
   floats; tiles are white on a stone paper #F3F1EB.
2. Page 1, row 1: the TITLE tile (4 cols — label, Outfit 700 at 40px, standfirst) and
   THE CALL tile (2 cols, red, the number or the name at 64px and the verdict's lead
   sentence). Row 2: the chapter's photograph (3 cols) beside the LEDGER AS A STACK
   (3 cols — one tile per option, gold for the chosen, stone for the rest, with the
   call in mono). Row 3: two PROSE tiles, two paragraphs each.
3. Page 2: a PULL tile (blue, a sentence from the prose at 24px, verbatim) beside the
   remaining prose and the verdict's reasoning; the FORKS as two tiles; the second
   photograph (4 cols) beside the VOLATILE tile (ink, gold label, ◆).
4. On a map chapter the map is a white tile at 3 columns with the option stack beside
   it in blue, and the photographs come as 2–3 column tiles with a caption bar.

**Type.** Outfit (display, labels) · Manrope (text) · IBM Plex Mono (calls).
**Colour.** Red #A23520 (the call), gold #E0A828 (chosen options), blue #0E6390
(pulls, the map's stack), ink #1B2622 (volatile), stone #DDE1DC (unchosen), white
tiles on paper #F3F1EB.

**She refuses.** A tile with two jobs; a rule or a border; a row whose height was
not decided; a colour used for two different jobs on one page.

**Where she is weakest.** Fixed tile heights clip: a stack tile that is one line
too short loses its call (caught on d06 at 262px; the row needs 286). Every chapter
must be composed row by row, and a chapter with seven paragraphs needs a third page.
The look is the closest of the five to a web layout and must stay printed-looking:
no shadows, no radii, the grout never wider than 8px.

**Pages per decision.** 2. **Specimen.** `build/models/m8-mosaique/`,
`atelier-visions-2026-10-24/Barcelona-vision-8-mosaique.pdf`.
