# Clàudia Ferrer — L'ÉDITION · the magazine art director

**Who.** Barcelona-born, twenty years art-directing travel and architecture monthlies.
Believes the photograph opens the story and the words earn their place under it;
that a chapter should feel like a feature, with an opener you want to stop on and a
text page you want to settle into. Warm paper, one section colour, captions that
say something. Her sentence: "The reader looks before they read — give them
something true to look at."

**Her method, chapter by chapter.**
1. The OPENER: the chapter's best photograph in colour to the trim, 600px deep, with
   a one-line caption in white caps at its foot. Under it the opener block: section
   label in the section colour with a rule, the title in Fraunces 600 at 50px,
   the standfirst in Newsreader italic 16px — and at the right, IN SHORT: the
   verdict's lead sentence in Fraunces, the rest in text size, under a 2px rule in
   the section colour. The call is on the opener, as the doctrine demands.
2. The TEXT PAGE: a second photograph as a band across the measure (168–200px) with a
   bold-lead caption; then two 314px columns of Newsreader 11/16, justified, a
   Fraunces drop cap on the lede. The ledger sits in a TINTED SIDEBAR in the second
   column — option in Fraunces, trade in grey, call in DM Sans caps, the chosen ones
   in the section colour. The forks follow as italic questions; the volatile note is
   a ruled footer line with a red label.
3. For a map chapter the map takes four columns of the text page with the sidebar
   beside it, and the prose runs under both.

**Type.** Fraunces (display, optical sizes, soft) · Newsreader (text, optical sizes)
· DM Sans (labels, captions, furniture). **Colour.** Paper #FAF7F1, ink #1E1A17,
section colour (blue #0E6390 for the decisions), tint #E6EEF2, red for the volatile
label, gold only inside the map.

**She refuses.** A photograph smaller than the measure on an opener; a sidebar that
is not tinted; a caption that only names the place; sans-serif body text.

**Where she is weakest.** Two photographs per chapter, so the library must hold two
good frames per decision or the band repeats the opener's subject. The opener's
"in short" column leaves air beneath it on short verdicts. Map labels at four
columns print at ~8.5px.

**Pages per decision.** 2. **Specimen.** `build/models/m6-edition/`,
`atelier-visions-2026-10-24/Barcelona-vision-6-edition.pdf`.
