Ten days in Bali gives room for a third stop, and for most travelers the best use of it is a few nights on the Nusa islands. A workable shape is three nights in Ubud, three on Nusa Lembongan or Nusa Penida, and four at a beach base in the south. The island leg belongs in the middle, with the boat well clear of the flight home.

What ten days adds

Ten nights are three more than a week, and those three nights are what make a third base possible. A week covers Ubud and one coast base. Ten days add one more region without rushing the first two. The Nusa islands, off the southeast coast, are the closest addition, since boats leave from Sanur.

Why the islands

The three Nusa islands offer what the mainland's south does not: small-island days with very little traffic, cliffs, snorkeling and surf. Nusa Lembongan is about 8 square kilometers and nearly free of cars. Nusa Penida is far larger and wilder. Tiny Nusa Ceningan is joined to Lembongan by a bridge.

A ten-day route

The ten-day route below adds an island leg to the classic pairing of Ubud and the coast. It is a suggestion to adapt, with no prices and no drive times. It assumes ten nights and puts the boat crossings on days four and seven, away from both flights.

  • Days 1 to 3: Ubud.
  • Day 4: drive to Sanur, boat to Nusa Lembongan.
  • Days 5 and 6: Lembongan, Ceningan and a day on Nusa Penida.
  • Day 7: boat back to Sanur, transfer to a southern beach base.
  • Days 8 to 10: the coast.
  • Day 11: depart.

Days 1 to 3: Ubud

Ubud comes first, while jet lag wakes you early. It is about 22 miles from the airport. Walk the center on the first full day and take a driver to the rice terraces and temples on the second. Ubud is also about 13 miles from Sanur's harbor, which makes it a convenient place to leave from.

Days 4 to 6: the islands

Boats to the Nusa islands leave mainly from Sanur. Travel guides give the crossing as roughly 30 to 45 minutes and note that most boats to Lembongan involve a wet landing, wading from the beach. Stay on Lembongan and visit Penida from there; guides put that hop at about ten minutes by local boat.

Days 7 to 10: the coast

Return to Bali with three or four nights left, and spend them at a beach base that suits you: Seminyak, Canggu, the Bukit or Sanur itself. Sanur has the advantage of needing no transfer after the boat. These last days carry no boats and no long drives.

Why the boat goes in the middle

The island leg sits in the middle of this route for one reason. Boat crossings depend on sea conditions, and we have no official source on how often they are delayed or cancelled. A boat on day seven leaves several days of slack before the flight. A boat on day ten leaves none.

What travel guides say about boats

Travel guides advise against boarding any fast boat you have doubts about, whether because of the vessel, overloading or the weather. Take a morning boat, in daylight. Keep phones and passports in a dry bag, and carry luggage you can lift, since a wet landing means wading with it.

Lembongan or Penida for the nights

The two main islands suit different travelers. Lembongan is small, easy and built for visitors, with a choice of places to stay. Penida is about 247 square kilometers, with rough roads and simpler services. Our view is that Lembongan is the better base for a first island stay.

What to do there

Two full days on the islands cover a snorkeling trip, the cliffs of Lembongan's south coast, the bridge to Ceningan, and a day on one side of Penida. Travel guides warn of rip currents at Dream Beach and of fatal falls at Devil's Tear; stay behind the barriers.

Alternatives to the island leg

The islands are one way to use three extra nights, and not the only one. A traveler uneasy on boats can add East Bali instead: Sidemen is about 22 miles from Ubud, and Amed about 49. A traveler who wants mountains can add Munduk, about 39 miles from Ubud.

Choosing among them

These are our own suggestions. Choose the islands for sea, snorkeling and beaches. Choose Sidemen for rice terraces and walking without crowds. Choose Amed for diving from the shore. Choose Munduk for waterfalls and cool air. Each replaces the island leg in the middle of the route.

Practical points

A ten-night stay is well inside the 30 days the tourist visa allows. The visa costs IDR 500,000 and Bali's tourist levy is IDR 150,000 per person. Bring cash to the islands and any medicine you rely on, since both are harder to come by there.

Insurance

The State Department strongly recommends insurance that covers medical evacuation in Indonesia, and says an injured traveler will likely need to reach a major city for care. From an island, that begins with a boat. Check how your policy treats boat travel, snorkeling and motorbikes.

Where the slack goes

Ten days leave room for one spare day, and where you put it matters more than whether you have it. How the island leg, the spare day and the departure are spaced is set out in the complete Bali trip guide.

FAQ

Is 10 days enough for Bali?

Yes. Ten nights cover Ubud, a beach base and one more region, such as the Nusa islands.

How should I split 10 days in Bali?

Our suggestion is three nights in Ubud, three on the Nusa islands and four at a southern beach base.

Should I stay on Nusa Lembongan or Nusa Penida?

Our view is Lembongan for a first island stay, with a day visit to Penida, which is about ten minutes away by local boat.

When in the trip should I do the islands?

In the middle. Boats depend on the sea, so keep several days between the return crossing and your flight home.
