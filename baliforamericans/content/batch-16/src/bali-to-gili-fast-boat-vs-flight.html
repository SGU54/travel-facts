From Bali to the Gili Islands, the fast boat is the direct route and the flight via Lombok is the steadier one. A fast boat from Padang Bai crosses in roughly an hour and a half, according to travel guides, and depends on the sea. Flying means a short flight to Lombok, a drive of well over an hour and a half, and a short boat. Each takes most of a day door to door.

The two routes

The Gili Islands lie off the northwest coast of Lombok, the island east of Bali, and a traveler has two ways to reach them. One is by sea the whole way, on a fast boat from Bali. The other is by air to Lombok's international airport, then by road to the harbor area at Bangsal, then by a short boat.

Why neither is fast

The crossing or the flight is the short part of either route. The rest is getting to a port or an airport, waiting, and getting from the far end to your hotel. We give no total door-to-door times, since they depend on your base and the day. Plan on the journey taking most of a day either way.

The fast boat

Fast boats run from several points on Bali. Travel guides name Padang Bai, in East Bali, as the main port, with a crossing of roughly an hour and a half. Boats from the south of the island take longer, around two and a half hours by the same guides, and Amed, on the northeast coast, has the shortest sea route.

Getting to the port

Padang Bai is about 35 miles from the airport by road, about 32 from Seminyak and about 23 from Ubud. From the southern resorts, the drive to the port is a journey in itself and usually starts early. Many operators sell a ticket that includes a hotel pickup; confirm the pickup time and place in writing.

What the crossing is like

Travel guides describe the boats as small high-speed craft, and the route crosses the open strait between Bali and Lombok. On a calm day it is a quick ride. In swell it is rough, and travel guides describe the sea as roughest in July and August. Anyone prone to seasickness should prepare for it.

What travel guides warn about

Travel guides are direct on safety. They note that crew training and safety standards vary considerably between operators, and they advise travelers who have concerns about the vessel, the operator, overloading or the weather not to board. They also advise against crossing at night. We name no operators.

Flying via Lombok

The air route replaces the open-sea crossing with a short flight and a drive. You fly from Bali to Lombok's international airport, drive across the island to the harbor area at Bangsal or Teluk Nare, and take a short boat to the islands. Travel guides put the drive at about an hour and forty minutes to two hours.

What it involves

This route has more stages: a drive to Bali's airport, a check-in, the flight, baggage reclaim, the drive, and the boat. Each stage is ordinary, and together they fill the day. The final boat is a short hop across sheltered water, a different thing from the crossing between Bali and Lombok.

Baggage on the flight

A domestic flight has its own baggage rules. According to Indonesian press reporting on the Transport Ministry's regulation, a full-service airline includes up to 20 kilograms of checked baggage, a medium-service airline up to 15, and a no-frills airline may charge for it. Check the fare before buying, and weigh your bags.

How to choose

The choice between boat and flight comes down to how you feel about small boats, the month you are traveling and where on Bali you start. These are our own suggestions, and they are a starting point, not a rule.

  • Choose the boat if you are starting from East Bali or Ubud, the sea is calm and you travel light.
  • Choose the flight in July and August, in the wet season, or if you get seasick.
  • Choose the flight if you are uneasy about small fast boats for any reason.
  • Choose the flight if you want to see Lombok as well.

From where you start

Your base on Bali tips the balance. From Amed or Sidemen, the port is close and the boat is the natural choice. From Uluwatu or Canggu, the drive to Padang Bai is long, and the airport is nearer, which makes the flight more competitive than it first appears.

One way each

The two routes can be combined. Some travelers take the boat out, when a delay costs only beach time, and fly back, when a delay would matter more. That gives one sea crossing for the experience and a steadier return.

The return is the leg that matters

Whichever route you choose, the return to Bali is the leg with consequences. We have no official source on how often boats are cancelled, and a domestic flight can be delayed too. Our advice is to be back on Bali at least one full day before an international flight, and two days in July and August.

Never same-day

A boat or a domestic flight from Lombok on the same day as your flight to the United States is a gamble on separate tickets. The US Department of Transportation's advice for any connection is to ask what happens if the first leg is delayed. Here the honest answer is that you miss the flight home.

Practical points for either route

A few points apply whichever way you travel. There are no cars or motorbikes on the Gili Islands, so pack bags you can carry across sand. Bring cash and any medicine you rely on. The State Department says an injured traveler in Indonesia will likely need to reach a major city for care.

Notice what happened across all of this. Boat or flight looked like a question of comfort and price, and the answer kept depending on other things: which side of Bali you sleep on the night before, which month it is, and how many days stand between the return leg and the flight home. FAQ

How long is the fast boat from Bali to the Gili Islands?

Travel guides give roughly an hour and a half from Padang Bai and longer from the south of Bali. Check times with the operator.

Is it better to fly or take the boat to Gili?

The boat is more direct; the flight via Lombok avoids the open-sea crossing. Our advice is to fly in July, August, the wet season, or if you get seasick.

Can you fly directly to the Gili Islands?

No. You fly to Lombok's airport, drive to the harbor area at Bangsal, and take a short boat.

How far is Padang Bai from Ubud?

About 23 miles by road.
