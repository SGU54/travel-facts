The Mount Batur sunrise trek is a pre-dawn climb of an active volcano in the Kintamani highlands, timed to reach the top for sunrise. Travel guides put the climb at about two hours from the lakeside village of Toya Bungkah, and say a local guide is required. The mountain is about 1,700 meters high, it is cold at the top, and the day starts in the middle of the night.

What Mount Batur is

Mount Batur is an active volcano of about 1,700 meters, roughly 5,600 feet, standing inside a large caldera in the Kintamani area of northeast Bali. A crater lake, Lake Batur, lies beside it. The caldera rim at Penelokan is the main viewpoint, described by travel guides as best early in the day before cloud forms.

Where it is

Kintamani is in the highlands, about 27 miles from Ubud by road, about 45 from Seminyak and about 48 from the airport. Those distances explain the schedule: a traveler staying in the south has a long drive before the walking begins. From Ubud the drive is shorter by a wide margin.

An active volcano

Batur is an active volcano, and access depends on its status. We have no live source for volcanic alerts, so check the current situation with your guide or accommodation before you book, and again the day before. A trek that is closed for safety is closed.

What the night looks like

The Batur trek is a short night followed by a short climb. A sunrise at the summit means walking in the dark, and walking in the dark means being collected from your hotel well before dawn. We give no clock times, since they vary by season, starting point and operator; ask yours for the pickup time.

The pickup

The earlier part of the night is a drive. The further your base is from Kintamani, the earlier the car comes. A stay in Ubud, or in the Kintamani area itself the night before, shortens that drive and lengthens your sleep. Our advice is not to attempt it from the far south.

The climb

Travel guides describe the route from Toya Bungkah as taking about two hours to the summit, with more than half of it through forest. The upper part is on open volcanic ground. You walk by flashlight or headlamp, in a line with other groups. We rate no difficulty; it is a steady uphill walk at night on a mountain.

At the top

The summit is cold before sunrise. Travel guides describe Kintamani as much cooler than the coast and advise a sweater or jacket. Cloud decides the view, and no one can promise a clear morning. The descent is in daylight, and many treks end with a visit to the hot springs in Toya Bungkah.

The guide requirement

A guide is required for the Batur climb, according to travel guides, and guides belong to the local Association of Mount Batur Trekking Guides, which has its office in Toya Bungkah. The same guides warn that touts target visitors in the area, and suggest booking the climb in advance.

Booking

Most visitors book the trek as a package that includes transport, the guide and breakfast. We quote no prices. Ask what is included, how large the group is, and whether the guide is a member of the local association. A booking made through your accommodation gives you someone to hold to account.

What to ask the operator

These are our own suggestions.

  • What time is pickup, and how long is the drive from my hotel?
  • How many people are in the group?
  • Is a flashlight or headlamp provided?
  • What happens if the mountain is closed or the weather is bad?

What to bring

Packing for Batur is packing for a cold, dark, dusty walk. Our advice: closed shoes with grip, a warm layer and a windproof one, a headlamp if you own one, water, and a small snack. Leave valuables at the hotel. A light scarf or buff helps with dust on the way down.

Fitness and health

The trek asks for a moderate level of fitness and sure footing in the dark. Anyone with a heart or breathing condition, a knee problem or a fear of heights should think carefully. The State Department says an injured traveler in Indonesia will likely need to reach a major city for appropriate care, and a mountain is a long way from one.

Insurance

Check how your travel insurance treats trekking and volcano climbs. Some policies list hiking above a certain altitude or on active volcanoes among their exclusions. Ask before the trip, in writing.

When in the trip to do it

The Batur trek fits best in the middle of a trip. On the first days, jet lag from the United States makes a traveler wake early, which helps, and also leaves them short of sleep, which does not. The CDC advises allowing at least two days to adjust before anything important.

The day after

Our advice is to keep the rest of trek day free. You will be back at your hotel by late morning having been awake since the small hours. Booking a long drive, a transfer to a new base or an evening event for the same day makes for a hard day.

Season

The dry months, from about May to October, are the conventional choice for a sunrise climb, since the point is a clear view. Climate figures for south Bali do not describe the mountains, so we give no rainfall numbers for Kintamani. In the wet season, ask about recent conditions before committing.

If you would prefer not to climb

The view of Batur does not require the climb. The caldera rim at Penelokan looks across the crater lake to the volcano, and travel guides describe the view as spectacular early in the day. It is reached by road, about 27 miles from Ubud, and suits anyone who wants the scenery without the night walk.

The temple and the lake

Pura Ulun Danu Batur, on the caldera rim, is one of Bali's main water temples and part of the island's UNESCO-listed rice-terrace and water-temple landscape. A day in Kintamani that combines the viewpoint, the temple and lunch is a gentler alternative to the trek.

FAQ

How long does the Mount Batur sunrise trek take?

Travel guides put the climb at about two hours from Toya Bungkah. With the drive and the descent, it takes most of a night and a morning.

Do you need a guide for Mount Batur?

Yes, according to travel guides. Guides belong to the local Association of Mount Batur Trekking Guides.

Is it cold on Mount Batur?

Yes. Kintamani is much cooler than the coast, and a sweater or jacket is advised for the summit before sunrise.

How high is Mount Batur?

About 1,700 meters, roughly 5,600 feet.
