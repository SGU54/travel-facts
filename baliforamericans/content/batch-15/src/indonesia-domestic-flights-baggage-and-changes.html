Indonesian domestic flights follow different baggage rules from your international ticket, and the allowance depends on the kind of airline. Under Indonesian regulation, a full-service airline includes up to 20 kilograms of checked baggage in an economy fare, a medium-service airline up to 15, and a no-frills airline may charge for every checked bag. Read the fare conditions before you buy, and never connect a domestic flight tightly to your flight home.

When a Bali trip includes a domestic flight

A domestic flight enters a Bali trip when the itinerary leaves the island: to Lombok for the Gili Islands, to Labuan Bajo for Komodo, to Java, or onward to another part of Indonesia. These flights are short. The planning around them is where the trouble lies, since they are separate from the ticket that brought you from the United States.

A separate booking

A domestic flight bought independently is a separate contract from your international ticket. The US Department of Transportation's advice for any connection involving two airlines is to ask whether your bags will be transferred. On separate bookings, assume they will not, and that a delay on one flight is not the other airline's concern.

Baggage allowances

Indonesia's Transport Ministry sorts domestic airlines into three service groups, and the checked baggage included in an economy fare depends on the group. According to Indonesian legal and business press reporting on Regulation PM 185 of 2015, full-service airlines include up to 20 kilograms, medium-service airlines up to 15, and no-frills airlines may charge.

Why it catches Americans out

A traveler who flew to Bali with a generous international allowance may hold a domestic ticket that includes far less, or nothing. 20 kilograms is about 44 pounds and 15 kilograms about 33. Two large suitcases that crossed the Pacific without charge can cost extra on a short hop to Lombok.

Which airline is in which group

We do not list which airline falls in which group, because classifications and fare products change, and we could not open the regulation itself to confirm the current position. The airline's own fare conditions, shown before payment, are the authority. Look for the checked baggage figure on the fare you are choosing.

Buying baggage

Where baggage is charged, our advice is to buy it when you book, since adding it at the airport is the costly way. Weigh your bags before you leave the hotel. We quote no fees, because they vary by airline, route and when you pay.

Carry-on and special items

Carry-on allowances on domestic flights are set by each airline and are often smaller than on long-haul flights. We have no official figure to quote. Check the cabin allowance for your fare, and keep medicines, documents and valuables in the bag that stays with you.

Surfboards and dive gear

Surfboards, dive equipment and other sports gear are handled under each airline's own rules and are often charged separately. Ask the airline before booking if you are traveling with a board. A short domestic flight can cost more for the board than for the passenger.

Medicines and batteries

Keep prescription medicine in hand luggage in its original packaging, with the prescription. Indonesia's medicine rules apply on domestic flights as they do on arrival. Spare lithium batteries and power banks belong in the cabin on any flight, not in checked bags.

Changes, delays and cancellations

Change and refund rules on domestic flights depend on the fare you bought. The cheapest fares are commonly the least flexible. We have no official source on any airline's change fees or on passenger compensation rules for domestic delays in Indonesia, so we state none. Read the conditions on the booking page.

If your flight is delayed

The practical protection against a delay is time. A domestic flight that lands a few hours late is an inconvenience on a travel day in the middle of a trip. On the day of your international departure, the same delay can cost you the flight home.

Insurance

Check what your travel insurance says about missed connections between separately booked flights. Policies differ, and some cover a missed onward flight only when a minimum gap was left between the two. Ask before the trip, in writing.

Connecting to your flight home

The rule that matters most is to keep domestic flights away from your international departure. Our advice is to return to Bali at least one full day before your flight to the United States. A domestic flight and a long-haul flight on the same day, on separate tickets, is a risk with a very large downside.

The onward ticket

Indonesia's entry rules expect a visitor to hold an outbound ticket from Indonesia. A domestic flight does not leave Indonesia and does not meet that expectation. Your ticket out of the country is the one to show on arrival.

Nyepi

Flights in and out of Bali are affected one day a year. In 2027 Nyepi falls on Monday, March 8, 2027, and press reports of the airport operator's 2026 announcement describe regular flights halting for 24 hours. Avoid that date for any flight touching Bali, and confirm with the airline.

At the airport

Domestic and international flights at Bali's airport use different terminals, so allow time to get between them if your plans involve both. Our advice is to carry your passport for a domestic flight, as your identity document at check-in, and to keep it in your hand luggage.

Check in online

Our advice is to check in online where the airline offers it and to arrive at the airport with margin. The drive to the airport is the unpredictable part: it is about 22 miles from Ubud and 12 from Canggu, and drive times vary widely by hour.

FAQ

How much baggage is allowed on Indonesian domestic flights?

It depends on the airline's service group: up to 20 kg on full-service airlines, up to 15 kg on medium-service airlines, and no-frills airlines may charge. Check your fare.

Will my bags be checked through to my domestic flight?

Not on separate bookings. Plan to collect and re-check them, and ask the airlines.

Can I fly back to Bali and leave for the US the same day?

Our advice is no. Return to Bali at least one full day before your international flight.

Do I need my passport for a domestic flight in Indonesia?

Carry it. It is your identity document for check-in.
