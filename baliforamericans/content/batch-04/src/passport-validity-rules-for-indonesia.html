To enter Bali, a US passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date and have 2 blank pages. Both conditions are checked, and a traveler who does not meet them is refused entry. Check the expiry date and count the empty pages before you book flights, because renewing a passport takes longer than buying a ticket.

The six-month passport rule

Indonesia requires a visitor's passport to be valid for at least six months beyond the date of arrival. The State Department lists the requirement on its Indonesia page, and the US Consular Agency in Bali repeats it for all visitors to the island. A passport that is still valid on the day you land, but expires four months later, does not meet the rule.

Count from the day you land

The six months run from your arrival in Indonesia, so the date that matters is the one you land in Bali. A flight from the United States crosses the date line and lands on a later calendar date than it departs. Take your arrival date, add six months, and compare the result with the expiry date printed on your passport's photo page.

Why a close call is a bad idea

The US Consular Agency in Bali states the consequence plainly: Balinese immigration authorities will not allow you to enter the country if you do not meet the requirement. There is no fee that fixes a short passport at the border. If your passport is within a year of expiring, our advice is to renew it before you plan the trip.

The two blank pages rule

Indonesia also requires two blank pages in the passport, and the US Consular Agency in Bali adds a detail many travelers miss: the endorsement page at the back of a US passport does not count as a blank page. The two pages must be visa pages with nothing stamped or stuck on them.

How to count your pages

Open the passport and look for pages headed "Visas" that are completely empty. Pages headed "Endorsements" do not count toward the two. A page with a single small stamp in one corner is no longer blank. If you have traveled a great deal on this passport, do the count now instead of at the check-in desk.

If you are short of pages

A passport that has run out of visa pages has to be replaced, and that is done through the State Department's passport renewal process. Processing times change through the year, so check the State Department's passport pages for the current estimate and the expedited options before you set your travel dates.

What immigration asks to see on arrival

Indonesia's official visa finder lists the documents a US tourist presents to the immigration officer on arrival: a passport valid for at least six months and an outbound ticket from Indonesia. The passport rule and the ticket rule sit side by side in the same list, so prepare both.

The onward ticket

The outbound ticket is a ticket that leaves Indonesia, to any country. It should fall inside the stay your visa allows, which for the tourist visa is 30 days, extendable once for another 30 days. Keep the booking confirmation where you can show it without a data connection, such as a screenshot or a printout.

The arrival card uses the same passport

The arrival card that every traveler submits in the last 72 hours before landing asks for your passport details. Enter the number of the passport you will travel on. If you renew your passport after buying an e-VOA, the visa was issued against the old passport number, so renew first and buy the visa second.

Emergency and limited-validity passports

A US emergency passport does not work with the visa on arrival. The US Consular Agency in Bali says a traveler on a limited-validity passport needs a visa obtained before arrival, which means applying at an Indonesian embassy or consulate before the trip. Anyone issued an emergency passport shortly before departure should deal with this before flying.

If your passport is lost in Bali

A passport lost or stolen during the trip is replaced through the US Consular Agency in Bali, which can be reached at +62-361-233-605. A replacement creates a second problem, which is that your entry was recorded against the old passport. That is covered by the next rule.

Leave on the passport you arrived with

A traveler must leave Indonesia with the same passport used to enter. The State Department says that if your passport is replaced during the trip, you must obtain a special pass from Indonesian immigration authorities before leaving. A new passport alone is not enough to board your flight home, so allow time for that step.

Dual nationals

The same-passport rule matters most to travelers who hold two passports. Enter and leave Indonesia on one of them, and buy the visa and fill in the arrival card with that passport's details. Mixing passports between the visa, the arrival card and the border is how records stop matching.

Keep a copy

Our advice is to keep a photo of your passport's photo page and of your visa on your phone, and a paper copy in a different bag from the passport. A copy is not a travel document, but it makes a report and a replacement application quicker to fill in.

A passport check to do before booking

A passport check for Bali takes four steps, and it belongs before the flight purchase. Find the expiry date; work out your arrival date and add six months; count the empty visa pages, leaving out the endorsement pages; and confirm the passport is a full-validity one. If any step fails, renew first.

Check every traveler

Each person in the group needs a passport that passes the same check, including children. A child's US passport is issued for a shorter period than an adult's, so a passport that felt new can be close to the six-month line. Look at every expiry date, not only your own.

Your connection country has rules too

There is no nonstop flight from the United States to Bali, so the trip passes through at least one other country. If you plan to leave the airport during a layover, that country's entry rules apply to your passport as well. Check them on the State Department's page for that country.

FAQ

How long does my passport need to be valid for Bali?

At least six months beyond your arrival date, according to the State Department and the US Consular Agency in Bali.

How many blank passport pages do I need for Indonesia?

Two. The US Consular Agency in Bali notes that the endorsement page at the back of the passport does not count as a blank page.

Can I enter Bali on an emergency passport?

Not with a visa on arrival. A traveler on a limited-validity passport needs a visa obtained before arriving in Indonesia.

What if my passport is replaced while I am in Bali?

The State Department says you must obtain a special pass from Indonesian immigration before leaving, because you must exit on the passport you entered with.
