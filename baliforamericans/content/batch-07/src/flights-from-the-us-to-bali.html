No airline flies nonstop from the United States to Bali, so every trip includes at least one connection. The usual pattern is a long flight across the Pacific to an Asian hub, or east through the Gulf or Istanbul, followed by a shorter flight into Bali. The choice of hub, and whether both flights are on one ticket, shapes the journey more than the choice of airline.

Is there a direct flight to Bali from the US?

There is no nonstop flight between the United States and Bali. Schedule data for Bali's airport in October 2026 lists dozens of international routes and no US city among them. The longest nonstop flight into Bali on that list is from Istanbul, at about 13 hours. An American traveler changes planes somewhere along the way.

Why that matters

A trip with a connection has two flights that can run late and one airport where a bag has to change planes. The US Department of Transportation's advice for any connection is to check the time between flights and to ask yourself what happens if the first one is delayed. On a journey this long, that question deserves a real answer before you pay.

The main ways to route the trip

Flights from the United States to Bali fall into two broad routings. The first crosses the Pacific to a hub in East or Southeast Asia and continues south to Bali. The second heads east across the Atlantic to the Gulf or to Istanbul and continues from there. Which one is shorter depends on where in the United States you start.

Through East Asia

The October 2026 schedule lists nonstop flights to Bali from Tokyo and Seoul, at roughly seven to eight hours, and from Taipei and Hong Kong, at roughly five to six hours. For travelers on the West Coast, these hubs lie closest to the straight line to Indonesia.

Through Southeast Asia

Singapore is the closest large hub to Bali on that schedule, at about three and a half hours, with several airlines on the route. Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok and Manila are also listed with nonstop flights to Bali. A routing through one of these puts the long flight first and leaves a short final leg.

Through the Gulf or Istanbul

The schedule lists nonstop flights to Bali from Dubai and Doha, at about ten hours, and from Istanbul, at about 13 hours. From the eastern United States, flying east can be about as short as flying west. This routing splits the journey into two long flights.

One ticket or two

How the flights are ticketed matters as much as where they connect. The Department of Transportation ranks itineraries by how safely bags travel: a nonstop is safest, then a through flight, then a connection on one airline, and last a connection between two different airlines. It advises asking whether bags will be transferred on any connection involving two airlines.

What we recommend

Our advice for a first trip is to book the whole journey to Bali as a single ticket, so that one booking covers both flights. If you build the trip from two separate bookings to save money, ask each airline in advance what happens to you and to your bag if the first flight arrives late. Do not assume the answer.

If a flight is delayed

The Department of Transportation notes that on international itineraries passengers may be able to recover expenses caused by a delay under the Montreal Convention, by filing a claim with the airline. It also points out that taking an earlier flight leaves more rerouting options if something goes wrong. Keep receipts for anything a delay costs you.

The time difference

Bali is on Central Indonesian Time, eight hours ahead of UTC, and Indonesia does not observe daylight saving time. That puts Bali 12 hours ahead of New York during daylight saving time and 13 hours ahead in winter, and 15 or 16 hours ahead of Los Angeles. Day and night are close to reversed.

You lose a calendar day going out

Flying west across the Pacific crosses the International Date Line, so the calendar jumps forward. Depending on the routing, a departure on a Friday evening can land in Bali on a Sunday. Count your hotel nights from the local arrival date printed on the ticket, not from the day you leave home.

Before you book

Booking flights to Bali touches several rules that have nothing to do with airlines. Your passport must be valid for 6 months beyond your arrival date and have 2 blank pages, and immigration expects a visitor to hold an outbound ticket from Indonesia. A one-way ticket into Bali needs a second booking out.

Check the calendar

Bali has one day a year when the airport does not handle regular flights. In 2027 Nyepi falls on Monday, March 8, 2027, and press reports of the airport operator's 2026 announcement describe a 24-hour halt. Do not book an arrival or a departure for that date, and confirm with your airline if your dates are close to it.

The layover country has rules too

If your connection is long enough that you want to leave the airport, the entry rules of that country apply to you. Check the State Department's page for the layover country before you plan a day in the city. A transit that stays inside the terminal is a different matter from an entry.

What this decision actually sits inside

Notice what happened across all of this. Choosing a flight turned into choosing a hub, a ticket type and an arrival date, and each of those reaches into the trip itself. The hour you land decides whether the first night is spent near the airport or across the island. The day you land decides which hotel night you pay for and never sleep in.
