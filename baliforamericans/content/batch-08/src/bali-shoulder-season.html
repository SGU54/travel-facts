Bali's shoulder season is April to June and September to October: the months on either side of the July and August peak. South Bali's rainfall normals are low in those months, at roughly 2 to 4 inches, against about 1 inch in July. The trade is a slightly higher chance of rain in exchange for fewer visitors, and for most travelers it is a good one.

When the shoulder season is

The shoulder season in Bali falls in two windows, one before the peak and one after it. The first runs from April to June, as the wet season ends. The second covers September and October, before the rains return in November. Both sit inside or beside the dry season on the rainfall figures.

The numbers behind it

Climate normals for the Denpasar station in south Bali show about 1 inch of rain in July and about 14 inches in January. The shoulder months sit near the bottom of that range: about 4 inches in April and May, under 3 in June, and about 2.5 in September and October.

Why the peak is the peak

July and August are the driest months in the normals, and they are also the summer vacation months in the United States, Europe and elsewhere in the northern hemisphere. Weather and school calendars point at the same eight weeks. The shoulder season is what remains of the dry season once those eight weeks are taken out.

What you gain

The gain from Bali's shoulder season is space. Fewer people are traveling, so the things that are limited in supply are easier to get: a room in the place you wanted, a driver on the day you need one, a table at sunset. We have no official occupancy or price statistics by month, so we describe the direction and not the size.

Temperature stays the same

Nothing is given up in warmth. The normals put the average temperature at the Denpasar station between about 79°F and 83°F in every month of the year. A shoulder-season day is as warm as a peak-season day, and the sea is as swimmable.

Green landscapes in the first window

April, May and June follow the wettest months of the year, when the south Bali normals show 9 to 14 inches of rain a month. Our observation, which is not a sourced fact, is that the landscape tends to look its greenest in the weeks after the rains, before the long dry stretch.

What you trade

The trade in the shoulder season is certainty about the weather. A normal of 4 inches in April is four times the July figure, and the seasons do not switch on a fixed date. Indonesia's meteorological agency forecasts the start of the dry season each year by zone, and for 2026 it predicted an earlier start than normal across most of Bali.

April and October are the edges

April and October are the months closest to the wet season on either side, so they carry the most uncertainty. In a year when the rains end late, April can still feel wet. In a year when they return early, late October can too. May, June and September sit further from the edges.

Check the calendar as well as the climate

The shoulder months contain their own busy days. Indonesian national holidays create long weekends that fill the island with domestic visitors, and the dates change yearly. In 2027, for example, Eid al-Adha falls on May 17 and Vesak on May 20, each with a collective leave day attached.

Which shoulder month for which trip

The two shoulder windows suit different trips, because of what comes before and after each. The first window follows the rains and leads into the peak; the second follows the peak and leads into the rains. The rainfall normals favor the second window slightly, at about 2.5 inches a month against 3 to 4.

May and June

May and June have falling rainfall in the normals, from under 4 inches to under 3. Surf guides describe the west-coast surf season as beginning around May. A traveler who must be home before American schools let out will find these the natural months.

September and October

September and October follow the driest months of the year. The normals show them at about 2.5 inches each, drier than May or June. Travelers without school-age children have the easiest time using this window, since it falls after the American summer.

April

April is the least certain of the shoulder months. The normals show about 4 inches of rain, a sharp drop from March's 9. Our advice is to treat April as shoulder season with a wet-season backup plan: keep a couple of days flexible and put the weather-dependent outings in the second half of the trip.

How to plan a shoulder-season trip

Planning for the shoulder season means leaving a little slack for weather, which costs nothing if the weather is fine. Our suggestions are simple: schedule boat trips and sunrise hikes early in the stay so there is room to move them, book the first nights and leave some later ones open, and pack a light rain jacket.

What to book ahead anyway

Fewer visitors does not mean none. Our advice is still to book flights early, since the journey from the United States involves a connection and the good itineraries go first, and to reserve anything with fixed capacity that your trip depends on.

What this decision actually sits inside

Notice what happened across all of this. Picking a shoulder month looked like a question about rain and crowds, and it turned out to depend on the school calendar at home, the holiday calendar in Indonesia, the coast you want to surf and how much slack your itinerary has. The month is one input among several, and they adjust each other.

FAQ

When is shoulder season in Bali?

April to June and September to October, the months on either side of the July and August peak.

Does it rain in Bali in May?

Some. South Bali's climate normals show a little under 4 inches of rain in May, compared with about 14 in January and about 1 in July.

Is September a good time to visit Bali?

Yes. The normals show about 2.5 inches of rain in September, and it falls after the northern summer vacation peak.

Is Bali cheaper in shoulder season?

We have no official price statistics by month. Fewer visitors generally means more availability, which is the advantage we can state.
