Every volatile figure in one place, dated. When this disagrees with the book, this is right and the book is old.

This sheet expires. The next one is free.

Issued 2026-10-15. Reissued every quarter. To pull the current one, go to baliforamericans.com/guide/sheet and enter the email you bought the manual with.

This sheet is the short half of a two-document set. The book argues the decisions and is built to stay true for about two years; this sheet carries the numbers those arguments run on and is reissued every quarter. Print it, or keep it on the phone — it is the half you want with you at a counter.

Checked 2026-10-15. Each row names the source it was read from, as a clickable address, and the decision it can reverse. A row marked confirm in your travel week is a figure no primary source publishes as a schedule: the sheet tells you where to look rather than printing a number it cannot stand behind.
