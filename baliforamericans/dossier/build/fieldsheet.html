Entry

Visa
Visa: required. B1 tourist visa (visa on arrival = e-VOA, same product), 30 days, extendable once for 30. Buy on the official site after flights and the final passport, or at the airport counter.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 04: the thirty-day outer edge.
Visa fee
Visa fee: 500000 IDR, same online or at the counter; the dollar amount moves with the rate. Online: Visa, Mastercard, JCB; one application covers up to five travellers. A higher price means an agency.
https:/​/​www.imigrasi.go.id/​biaya_imigrasi/​index
e-VOA validity
e-VOA validity for entry: 90 days from issue, so it can be bought well ahead; the form asks for a departure date and a booked return flight.
https:/​/​evisa.imigrasi.go.id/​web/​visa-selection
Before you fly: buy after the flights and the final passport.
Extension
Extension: once, 30 days; online application and payment, then a photograph and interview at an immigration office (since 29 May 2025). Fee 500000 IDR per application. No official processing time is published: the site's margin is to file about two weeks before day 30.
https:/​/​imigrasi.go.id/​siaran_pers/​2025/​05/​28/​tekan-angka-pelanggaran-keimigrasian-wna-wajib-ke
Decision 04 and 08: the office visit, and the holiday clusters.
Overstay
Overstay: 1000000 IDR per day (State Dept), with possible detention, deportation or a ban. Thirty days is counted from the stamp, not rounded; read the date on the record before leaving the desk.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 04.
Passport
Passport: six months beyond the landing date; two blank VISA pages (endorsement pages do not count). An emergency or limited-validity passport cannot use the visa on arrival: apply at an embassy before travel.
https:/​/​id.usembassy.gov/​bali/​
Before you fly: renew first.
Onward ticket
Onward ticket: immigration can ask for a ticket out of Indonesia; a domestic hop to Lombok does not count. Carry the booking offline.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 09: a one-way into Bali needs its second booking.
Leaving
Leaving: on the same passport you entered with. A passport replaced during the trip needs a special pass from immigration before you can fly; allow time. US Consular Agency, Jimbaran: +62-361-958-6161.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Tourist levy
Levy: 150000 IDR per person, once per trip (a Lombok or Gili side trip stays inside it). Pay at lovebali.baliprov.go.id by card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB) before you fly; voucher QR by email, one per traveller, saved offline. No cash anywhere. Exemptions are by visa type; none for children found.
https:/​/​lovebali.baliprov.go.id/​
Decision 04: the second system; carried to the temple gate.
Arrival card
Arrival card (All Indonesia): free; opens only within 72 h of the LOCAL arrival date; one per traveller including infants; carries the customs and health declaration; QR code to immigration and customs. A site that charges is not the official one.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 04: the step that cannot be done early.
Customs
Customs, per adult: USD 500 of goods duty-free (10% duty + VAT above); 1 L alcohol (21+); 200 cigarettes / 25 cigars / 100 g; vape liquid 30 ml open or 12 ml closed (18+). EXCESS ALCOHOL, TOBACCO AND VAPES ARE DESTROYED, NOT TAXED. Duty-free bought at the hub counts. Medicine → red channel with the prescription.
https:/​/​www.beacukai.go.id/​barang-penumpang
Decision 04: duty-free bought at the hub counts.
Cash declaration
Cash declaration: 100000000 IDR or equivalent, in and out; undeclared penalty 10%, max 300000000 IDR. Taking that much RUPIAH out needs a Bank Indonesia permit. Spend or change small notes before leaving.
https:/​/​www.beacukai.go.id/​faq-impor-pembawaan-uang-tunai
Decision 11.
Phone
Phone: no IMEI registration for a tourist roaming on a US SIM or using a local SIM registered at an operator's outlet (90 days' access). A foreign travel eSIM is the site's reading of the roaming case, not a customs statement. The phone must be unlocked for either.
https:/​/​www.beacukai.go.id/​faq-impor-registrasi-imei

Medicine and law

Medicine
Medicine: prescribed, 90 days with the prescription; psychotropic, 60 days; NARCOTIC CLASS, NONE (prescription or not); over-the-counter, three days' need. Original packaging, prescription per item, a letter by generic name. Declare it on the arrival card; the red channel is where it is looked at. BPOM publishes a contact for individual questions.
https:/​/​sertifikasicdob.pom.go.id/​wasdisobat/​faq/​
Decision 10: the first link.
ADHD and stimulants
ADHD and other stimulants: the State Dept says some, including ADHD medicines, are illegal; no official list exists. Ask your doctor which class your drug is in and what you would switch to.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 10.
Cannabis and CBD
Cannabis and CBD: illegal in every form, Class I under the Narcotics Law; importing carries 5–15 years and, above 1 kg of plant or 5 g in other forms, life or death. Check jacket pockets and toiletry bags before packing.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
The risk chapter.
Driving
Driving: US licence alone not recognised; carry an IDP (AAA, issued at home) WITH the licence. Motorcycles are a separate licence class: an IDP translates a car licence and adds nothing. The site's reading: no motorcycle endorsement = unlicensed on a scooter.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 05.
Riding unlicensed
Riding unlicensed: up to 4 months' detention or 1000000 IDR (statutory maxima, not a roadside price). Helmets required for rider and passenger. If fined: ask for an official receipt (State Dept).
https:/​/​jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/​api/​download/​fulltext/​2009/​22TAHUN2009UU.htm
Decision 05.
After an accident
After an accident: stay at the scene, notify the police; the larger vehicle's driver is often presumed responsible. The police report is what a claim will ask for.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 05 and 10: the police report.
Bali's rules
Circular 7/2025: licensed accommodation only; modest dress at sacred places; inner and middle temple courtyards for worshippers in Balinese dress only; licensed changers, pay in rupiah; licence + helmet to ride; pay the levy; no single-use plastics; roadworthy registered hire cars. Residents report via a WhatsApp hotline.
https:/​/​bmc.baliprov.go.id/​news/​11462
The set pieces: temples, changers, accommodation.
Enforcement
Enforcement: the US Consular Agency reports an active campaign on traffic and vehicle violations, illegal work and visa violations, and drug possession by foreigners. The rules above are being applied.
https:/​/​id.usembassy.gov/​bali/​
The risk chapter.

Safety and health

Advisory
Travel advisory: Level 2 — Exercise Increased Caution (Indonesia as a whole; Level 4 for two Papua provinces). Read it again before you fly; advisories are revised.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
The risk chapter.
Care and billing
Care: deposit before treatment, mostly cash; US plans, Medicare and Medicaid pay nothing abroad; insurance reimburses from the medical report, itemised bill, proof of payment and police report. Ask the clinic for an estimate; no price is printed here because none is official.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 10: the third link.
Emergency numbers
Emergency: 112 unified (free, works without credit) · 110 police · 119 ambulance (State Dept also lists 118) · 113 fire. Ambulances can take 45 min or more: a taxi is the ambulance. Ask your accommodation on day one which clinic it would send a guest to.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Evacuation
Evacuation: strongly recommended by the State Dept; transfers between hospitals are the patient's cost. Read what triggers it, who decides and where it takes you; check the scooter, diving, trekking and boat clauses by name.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 10: the clause to read first.
Rabies
Rabies: common in Indonesia's dogs. After ANY bite or scratch: soap and running water, then a clinic the same day, and call the insurer. Pre-travel vaccine: a doctor's decision for remote travel, small children or long stays.
https:/​/​wwwnc.cdc.gov/​travel/​destinations/​traveler/​none/​indonesia
Decision 10: the decision made at home.
Malaria and dengue
Malaria: none in Bali's resort areas, Ubud or the Gilis (CDC); present elsewhere in Indonesia. Dengue: everywhere, bites by day, no US vaccine; warning signs come 24–48 h AFTER the fever breaks.
https:/​/​wwwnc.cdc.gov/​travel/​destinations/​traveler/​none/​indonesia
Before you fly.
Zika
Zika: CDC Level 2 travel health notice for Bali at this edition. Pregnant or planning: read the current notice and talk to a doctor before booking. Notices change; check the date.
https:/​/​wwwnc.cdc.gov/​travel/​destinations/​traveler/​none/​indonesia
Before you fly.
Vaccines
Vaccines: none required at the border. CDC recommends routine, hepatitis A, hepatitis B and typhoid; Japanese encephalitis only for long or rural stays. Some need spaced doses: 6–8 weeks out.
https:/​/​wwwnc.cdc.gov/​travel/​destinations/​traveler/​none/​indonesia
Before you fly: the clinic deadline.
Water
Water: sealed bottled water from a major brand (State Dept); ask for it by name and check the seal; no ice where unsure; brush teeth with bottled water. A filter bottle squares this with the plastics rule.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Drinks
Drinks: methanol poisoning and spiking are both reported (State Dept). Sealed, branded, opened in sight; spirits from a labelled bottle only; nothing from strangers; nothing left unattended. A hangover out of proportion, or any change in vision: hospital, and say methanol.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
The risk chapter.
US Consular Agency
US Consular Agency, Jimbaran: +62-361-958-6161 (lost passport, arrest, consular help). Local emergency numbers first for medical or safety emergencies. Embassy: Jakarta.
https:/​/​id.usembassy.gov/​bali/​

Getting there

Nonstop
Nonstop US–Bali: none (October 2026 schedule). Every routing connects once; book both flights on one ticket for a first trip.
https:/​/​www.flightconnections.com/​flights-from-denpasar-dps
Decision 09.
Hubs
Hubs, second leg into Bali (Oct 2026 snapshot): Singapore ~3.5 h · KL / Bangkok / Manila listed · Hong Kong, Taipei ~5 to 6 h · Tokyo, Seoul ~7 to 8 h · Dubai, Doha ~10 h · Istanbul ~13 h. Routes change by season: search your own dates.
https:/​/​www.flightconnections.com/​flights-from-denpasar-dps
Decision 09: the shortest final leg.
Time
Time: UTC+8, no clock change. +12 h on New York in summer, +13 in winter; +15/+16 on Los Angeles. The flight west crosses the date line: count hotel nights from the LOCAL arrival date on the ticket.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 01: the calendar day lost.
Two tickets
Two tickets: ask the second airline, before buying, what it does for a passenger stranded by the first airline's delay; assume bags are NOT transferred (collect, enter the hub country, re-check). Delay expenses: a Montreal Convention claim afterwards, with receipts.
https:/​/​www.transportation.gov/​airconsumer/​fly-rights
Decision 09.
Domestic flights
Domestic flights: 20 kg full-service / 15 kg medium / no-frills may charge (PM 185/2015 as reported); the fare conditions are the authority. Separate ticket from your international one; never on the same day as the flight home. Different terminals at Denpasar.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​indonesia-domestic-flights-baggage-and-changes

The island

Road miles
Road miles from the airport: Kuta 4 · Seminyak 7 · Nusa Dua 8 · Sanur 11 · Canggu 12 · Uluwatu 13 · Ubud 22 · Padang Bai 35 · Kintamani 48 · Munduk 51 · Amed 61 · Lovina 62. Drive times: none printed; a live map at your hour and day, the night before, and the longer of its answer and your driver's.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​bali-traffic-real-drive-times
Decision 02 and 07; the last night's drive.
Nights and good days
Nights → good days (the site's estimate): 5 → ~3, one base · 7 → ~5, two · 10 → ~8, three · 14 → ~12, four. Ten nights is the site's recommended first-trip length from the US.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​how-many-days-in-bali
Decision 01.
Rain
Rain, inches, Denpasar normals: Jan 14 · Feb 11 · Mar 9 · Apr 4 · May <4 · Jun <3 · Jul 1 · Aug 1.4 · Sep 2.5 · Oct 2.5 · Nov 5 · Dec 13. 79–83°F all year. Sea level only; Ubud, the mountains and the north are not described. The meteorological agency forecasts each season's start by zone: read it in your travel month.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​best-time-to-visit-bali
Decision 03.
Surf coast
Surf: west coast and the Bukit April–October (biggest swells June–August); east coast (Keramas, Sanur, Nusa Dua) November–March. A base follows the coast. Beginners: a school chooses the beach and the hour; many Bukit waves break over reef.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​surfing-in-bali-by-season-and-coast
Decision 03: the base follows the coast.
Crossings
Crossings (travel-guide figures, no official timetable): Sanur–Penida ~30 min (Toyapakeh, Buyuk, Sampalan) · Sanur–Lembongan 35–45 min (Jungutbatu or Mushroom Bay; wet landing) · Lembongan–Penida ~10 min · Padang Bai–Gili ~1.5 h, Amed shortest, south ~2.5 h · Lombok airport–Bangsal 1 h 40–2 h by road. Morning boats, in daylight; a named boat and time; a life jacket per seat.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​fast-boats-to-nusa-penida-and-lembongan
Decision 06.
The sea and the buffer
The sea: sailing approvals are issued per boat by the harbour master, who holds boats in poor weather; waves above 2.5 m put the Sanur route at risk; a boat capsized at Sanur on 5 Aug 2025 (two deaths). Roughest: July–August. No cancellation rate exists. BUFFER: one full day before an international flight, two in July, August or the wet season.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​when-bali-fast-boats-cancel
Decision 02 and 06: where the boat sits.
Batur
Batur: ~2 h climb from Toya Bungkah, ~1,700 m, guide required (local association, office in Toya Bungkah); pickup well before dawn, earlier the further south you sleep; cold at the top; an active volcano, so confirm its status the day before. Prices: none printed; ask what the package includes.
https:/​/​www.baliforamericans.com/​article/​mount-batur-sunrise-trek
Decision 07.

Money

On the bill
On the bill: 10% local tax on food, drink and rooms in Badung and Gianyar (Denpasar's rate not sourced; Sanur: read the menu). Service charge: the business's own rate, to staff by regulation. Entertainment (bars, clubs, spas): 40% set from Jan 2024, contested and possibly amended — read how the venue states prices.
https:/​/​bapenda.badungkab.go.id/​
Decision 11.
QRIS
QRIS: not usable from a US banking app (connected: Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, China). Cards and rupiah cash are the two methods. The official sites take Visa, Mastercard, JCB (visa) and Amex too (levy); charged in rupiah.
https:/​/​www.bi.go.id/​id/​fungsi-utama/​sistem-pembayaran/​ritel/​kanal-layanan/​qris/​qris-antarnegara
Decision 11: cards and cash only.
Changers and ATMs
Changers: licence number + Bank Indonesia QR logo at the counter, or no deal; agree the amount, count it yourself, be the last to touch the stack. ATMs: US cards accepted, fees possible (State Dept); secure locations, check for tampering; no official limit or fee figure exists.
https:/​/​bmc.baliprov.go.id/​news/​11462
Decision 11.
ATM fee and cap
ATM fee and cap: not printed — none is primary-sourced. Read the machine's screen; your own bank's foreign schedule is the half you can check before you fly. Decline the dollar offer; pay in rupiah.
https:/​/​travel.state.gov/​en/​international-travel/​travel-advisories/​indonesia.html
Decision 11: the rule stands whatever the figure.
Prices
Prices: confirm in your travel week — a driver's day rate, hours, fuel, parking and stops in writing; entrance fees at the booth, small notes; boat tickets with the cancellation terms; beach-club minimum spends per person or per bed, before or after tax. The book prints none of these, on purpose.
