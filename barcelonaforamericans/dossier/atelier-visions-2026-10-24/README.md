# Five art directors — Barcelona, two chapters (2026-10-24)

Models 1 and 2 changed the method and kept one look (L'Entrada, the ticket). The owner
asked for other hands. Five named directors composed the same two chapters, with the
same words and the same photo library:

- **d06 · The Gaudí Fork** — a photograph decision (two frames in the library)
- **d01 · Where to Base** — a map decision (the neighbourhood figure + one aerial)

| file | director | vision | pages |

Each `*-DIRECTOR.md` is the director's file from the skill (who, method, type, colour,
what they refuse, where they are weakest). Each `*-contact.png` is their pages in a row.

`eye/d06.png` and `eye/d01.png` sit model 2 (top row) above the five directors — the
same chapter, the same scale. `eye/five-hands-d06-p1.png` is the first page of the
Gaudí chapter in the five hands side by side. The question per row is the Eye's:
*can you tell what was decided on this page, and by what hand?*

## 5b · Marta Serra beyond the decisions (2026-10-24, evening)

`Barcelona-vision-5b-affiche-essais-outils.pdf` — the chapters with no verdict, in her hand
(source `build/models/m5b-affiche-essays/`). Her rule for the book: **colour is a verdict,
paper is an argument.**
- **The Ground** (essay, 4 pp.): a PAPER poster with the thesis at 64px (the one red thing is
  "four small times"), a text page with the four charges as a stack, FIG. 1 redrawn at 1:1 on
  the measure, a photograph page closing on the last paragraph.
- **The Plan** (tool, 3 pp.): the poster IS the tool — five days as five columns, fixed days
  in red fields, floating days on paper; then the three rules as a stack; then the day sheet
  with 104px numerals.
- **The Wallet Card** (tool, 1 p.): the two rules at 30px, the reference as one-line stacks,
  and a cut-out card (front red EUROS., back the street lines) at true card size.
