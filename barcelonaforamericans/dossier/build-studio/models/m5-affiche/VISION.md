# Model 5 — L'AFFICHE · the poster designer

**Who.** A Catalan poster designer in the Müller-Brockmann line by way of Barcelona '92.
Believes one page should say one thing, and say it so large that the reader decides
before reading. Colour is structure, never decoration: a red page, a paper page, a
photograph page. Type is huge or small, nothing in between. Air is deliberate.

**The chapter, in this hand.**
- The decision opens on a **poster page** (full sheet, no furniture): a field of colour
  carrying the verdict as a number or a word you can read across a room — "2+1" at
  330px for the Gaudí fork, "EIXAMPLE" at 150px for the base — a one-line gloss under
  it, and at the foot the verdict's lead sentence at 30px beside its reasoning in a
  300px column. The call is the first page, as the doctrine demands, and it is the
  loudest thing in the book.
- The **text page**: the title in Archivo 800 condensed at 58px, the standfirst in
  Archivo 300, then an asymmetric grid — prose in a single 340px column at 11/17
  ragged, and in the right column the **ledger as a stack**: each option at 19px bold
  with its call in red caps, the chosen ones marked with a red square, separated by
  rules. The **forks** continue the stack. No boxes anywhere: rules and type only.
- The **photograph page**: one frame per chapter, full bleed to three quarters of the
  sheet, cropped hard, with the chapter's last paragraph and the volatile note set
  under it as a tail. For d01 the map takes a page of its own at the full measure,
  with the ledger as a four-cell ribbon beneath it, and the photograph closes the text
  page as a band.

**Type.** Archivo variable (one family at every width and weight: 118 % wide for the
giant number, 88 % for titles, 92 % for the stack, 100 % for text). One family is the
poster's discipline.

**Colour.** Red #A23520 and gold #E0A828 as fields, paper #F4F2EC, ink, grey. On the
map the default is red and the sea is drawn in ink.

**What this vision refuses.** Boxes, tints, hairlines around things; more than one
photograph per chapter; a page that mixes a poster and prose; a type size between 20
and 50px.

**Where it is weakest.** It costs pages: three per decision instead of two, and the
poster pages are the first thing a buyer will flip past if the number is not a real
verdict (the gold EIXAMPLE page says the call; a decision whose answer is "it
depends" has no poster). The text pages carry ≈ 15 % air at the foot — the poster's
air, but the Eye must say whether it reads as composed or as short.

**Pages per decision.** 3.
