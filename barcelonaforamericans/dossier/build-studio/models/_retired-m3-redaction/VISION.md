# Model 3 — LA RÉDACTION · the broadsheet art director

**Who.** A design editor who spent twenty years on a Sunday broadsheet. Believes a
page is a front page and that hierarchy is the whole art: the reader must know in one
second what the biggest thing is, in five seconds what the story is, and only then
decide whether to read the body. Distrusts white space ("air is what you print when
you have nothing to say") and distrusts decoration more.

**The chapter, in this hand.**
- The **slug** (hairline over, 3px rule under) is the section bar of a newspaper: the
  decision number and the reader it is for. It replaces eyebrow + kicker.
- The **headline** is the chapter title, Playfair Display 900 at 64px, tight. It is the
  largest thing on the page and the only display setting.
- The **deck** is the standfirst, Source Serif italic 16.5px, across five columns.
- **THE CALL** is a boxed news summary beside the photograph: 3px rule, label, the
  verdict's lead sentence in Playfair 700, the rest in body size. It arrives before the
  body, as the doctrine demands, but as a sidebar rather than a ticket.
- The **body** runs in three 201px columns with hairline column rules, 10.5/14 justified
  and hyphenated, a drop cap on the lede. Chromium balances the columns.
- **Photographs** run once, large, in ink (grayscale + a warm sepia 14 %): a broadsheet
  prints news pictures, not atmosphere. One on page 1 beside the call, one as the
  closing plate.
- The **ledger** becomes AT A GLANCE: option in Playfair 700, trade in grey, call in
  Franklin caps right-aligned, the chosen rows in spot red. The **forks** are a second
  boxed column, "If you are not the default reader". The **volatile note** is a tinted
  corrections strip across the page.

**Type.** Playfair Display (display) · Source Serif 4 (text, optical size 10) · Libre
Franklin (labels, captions, furniture). Three faces, as the house allows.

**Colour.** Paper #FBFAF6, ink, one spot (#A23520) for the chosen rows and the volatile
label. Gold survives only inside the map.

**What this vision refuses.** Full-bleed photographs (they are posters, not news);
pull quotes (the deck already pulled); any page under 80 % full; a second display face.

**Where it is weakest.** The map is set at four columns and its labels fall to ~8.5px —
legible in a broadsheet, under the dossier's 12px printed floor. The two-page chapter
front-loads everything on page 1 and leaves page 2 as the instruments page, which a
reader may mistake for a new chapter without the slug repeating (it does not, by
choice: a newspaper jump page carries only the running head).

**Pages per decision.** 2.
