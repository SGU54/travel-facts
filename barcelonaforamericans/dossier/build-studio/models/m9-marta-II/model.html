Decision 01 · BaseWhere to Base
EIXAMPLE
First trip, no special agenda —
and it isn't close.
First trip, no special agenda: the Eixample, and it isn't close.

Central, quiet at night, deepest hotel stock, and it puts Gaudí's front doors on your walking route. Choose El Born only if atmosphere is the point and you sleep through noise; choose the beach only if you will actually be at the beach rather than commuting from it. Most first-timers who override the default are solving for a preference they do not really have.

Decision 02 · BaseThe Apartment Question
HOTEL
Or an apart-hotel. The apartment only with a HUTB number.
For most American first-timers in this climate, the honest call is a hotel or an apart-hotel — not the apartment.

What is left of the licensed market is pricier, books earlier, and sits in a pool increasingly padded with listings that should not exist. If you want a kitchen, the apart-hotel buys the feel without the licence roulette. Reserve the true apartment for when you have a valid HUTB number and real payment protection.

Decision 03 · MoneyThe Money-Trap Web
4
1 · BED TAX — BUDGET IT
2 · AIRPORT TICKET — BOTH WAYS
3 · EUROS — ALWAYS
4 · A BANK MACHINE — NOT A TOURIST ONE
Four gates, four moves. Five minutes to learn; they defend the week.
The four moves take five minutes to internalise and defend the better part of a couple's discretionary week.

Budget the bed tax so it cannot surprise you, buy the right airport ticket in both directions, say “euros” at every terminal, and pull cash from a bank machine. None of this is exotic and none of it needs an app — it needs knowing the four gates exist before you reach them. That is the entire edge: the charges are public, the pattern is not.

Decision 04 · TimeSequencing the Days
3
Three anchors. The rest floats.
Arrival day holds nothing timed.
Anchor three things and let everything else flow around them:

Sagrada Família on a morning slot, the day trip on a full clear day mid-trip so a grey forecast can still be traded, and the beach kept deliberately unpinned. Arrival day holds nothing timed. Do this and every sight lands in its good version; skip it and you will technically see everything while getting the lesser cut of half of it. The order is the product here — the list is free on the blog.

Decision 05 · TicketsThe Gaudí Fork
2+1
Two ticketed Gaudí sites and one house is the first-timer's sweet spot:

Sagrada Família in the morning, Park Güell on your slot, and exactly one of Casa Batlló or La Pedrera. That is a full, un-saturated dose. Everything else folds into the walks between them. The saturation trap costs attention and money at once — the fourth interior you paid for is the one you barely see.

Decision 06 · TimeWhich Day Trip
MONTSERRAT
It out-memories the city, and has no substitute within reach.
If you take one, take Montserrat.

It out-memories the city for most visitors, and the mountain-plus-monastery has no substitute within reach. Swap to Girona if you would rather have a medieval city and no climbing, or Sitges if what you want is a beach day with a change of scene. Figueres and Tarragona are specialist picks — superb for the Dalí devotee or the Roman-ruins person, underwhelming if you are neither. The wrong day trip is not ruinous; it is a day spent on someone else's ideal day.

Decision 07 · EatingHow Not to Eat Like a Tourist
20:30
Dinner on Spanish time — the rule that puts you in the right rooms.
Eat the menú del día at least once, order rice where it is cooked to order, keep your tapas off the tourist spine, and eat on Spanish time.

That is the defence entire, and three of the four rules cost you less while feeding you better — the tourist premium here buys a worse meal, which makes this the rare trap that is easy to refuse once you can see it. The city's food reputation is entirely earned. You simply have to order out of the local register rather than the one built for you.
