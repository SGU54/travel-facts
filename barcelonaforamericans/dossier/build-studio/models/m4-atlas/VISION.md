# Model 4 — L'ATLAS · the information designer

**Who.** An information designer in the Isotype and pilot-book tradition. Believes a
page is a chart: every element has a position, a scale and a key, and the reader
should be able to use the page without reading it. Quiet type, one signal colour,
numbered everything. Dislikes atmosphere ("a photograph that proves nothing is a
mood board").

**The chapter, in this hand.**
- The **header** is a sheet header: the decision number at 72px in signal red (the
  only large thing on the sheet), the title in Space Grotesk 500, a mono sub-line, and
  a **reading key** at the right ("§-numbered prose · ■ attribute applies · signal red
  = the call · PL. = evidence plate").
- The **abstract** is the standfirst in Inter Light 14.5px.
- The **hero is the figure**. Where the chapter has a map (d01) it runs at the full
  measure, recoloured into the atlas palette, with a mono "FIG. 01-A" caption and a
  **key** on the next sheet that doubles as the ledger. Where the chapter has none
  (d06) the AD *draws one from the ledger*: FIG. 05-A, an attribute matrix (ticket ·
  timed · interior) with a red bracket marking the dose. The ledger's own words become
  the call column; nothing is invented.
- The **prose** is numbered §1–§5 in the margin so a fork or a notice can cite it,
  set in two 314px columns, Inter 10.5/15, ragged.
- **THE CALL** is a signal block: 4px red rule at left, the verdict's lead sentence in
  Grotesk 17px.
- The **forks** are a two-column table (IF / THEN); the **volatile note** is a NOTICE
  box with a hatched red edge, the pilot-book "notice to mariners".
- **Photographs** are evidence plates: square (or 2:1), numbered PL. 05.1–3, in a strip
  at the foot of the sheet, each caption saying what the plate proves.

**Type.** Space Grotesk (display) · Inter (text) · IBM Plex Mono (apparatus) — the
house mono kept, because the apparatus is the dossier's.

**Colour.** Paper #F5F6F3, ink, grey #6B7773, panel #E8EDEA, one signal (#A23520).
The map keeps the sea blue and the gold default because they are data.

**What this vision refuses.** A photograph larger than a plate; any colour that is not a
key; prose that cannot be cited; a hero that is not a figure.

**Where it is weakest.** The drawn figure (05-A) is only as good as the ledger's
attributes — "timed" and "interior" were read from the ledger's own text, and a figure
that invents an attribute would be a lie in a chart's clothing. The sheet needs the
writer to supply attributes per option at Stage C. Page air at the foot of §4 (≈ 12 %)
is the atlas's quiet, not a defect, but the Eye should say whether it reads as quiet
or as unfinished.

**Pages per decision.** 2.
