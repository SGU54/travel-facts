# Writing code

Read with c-write.md. The manuscript and the generated PDF must say the same thing.

## Voice and structure

Use precise, natural English for an American reader unless the user chooses another
language or audience. State the recommendation early, then the reason and material
exception. Prefer a clear conditional sentence to an invented universal rule.
Qualify estimates and uncertainty where they affect the action; do not hedge routine facts.

Choose the structure for the job: a comparison for alternatives, steps for a sequence,
a checklist for preparation, prose for explanation. Do not force every topic into identical
“default / exception / trap” boxes. A factual rule need not become a contested decision.

Keep a consistent heading hierarchy, short informative table headings, and explicit units.
Use genuine scores only when the scale and scoring method are defined. “Scored,” “surveyed,”
“all the arithmetic,” and “tested” must describe work actually present in the product.

## Sources without clutter

Cite the consequential claim next to its sentence/table or with a clear source-note key.
Keep the source note reachable in the PDF. Reuse a note for multiple claims from the
same exact source when the scope remains clear.

Optional provenance labels may distinguish official statements, observations, estimates,
and editorial judgment. They do not establish truth, reliability, or freshness on their
own. Maintain one meaning per label and never reuse a provenance glyph as decoration.
Do not force the legacy glyph vocabulary on a product that uses ordinary source notes.

No claim of surveying without a method, sample, and dates. No broad “most people” claim
derived from reading the publisher's own articles. A useful heuristic should remain a
heuristic, with a named limit; a restaurant photograph cannot prove food was frozen.

## Durable content and current facts

The book can explain a durable decision and show a clearly dated worked example.
The Sheet holds the current values and conditions needed to apply it. Changing a rule
may change the reasoning too; update both. Repeated operational rules use canonical
claim bindings. Avoid freehand duplication of prices or eligibility exceptions.

## Final read

Read the source, then the final page: missing text, split words, table heads, caption
truth, displayed arithmetic, and a recap that gives the same action. Search for obsolete
phrases after every revision. Remove repetitive product claims and unfinished fragments.
Do not rewrite the text merely to hit a punctuation rate, word floor, or page quota.
